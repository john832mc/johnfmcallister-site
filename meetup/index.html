Eastern PA Creative REI Meetup in King of Prussia

Join investors from Eastern Pennsylvania and the Tri-State area for a free, in-person meetup focused on creative financing, Subject-To, wholesaling, pre-foreclosures, and real deal talk.

Next meetup: Saturday, December 12, 2026, from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM ET.

Place: Conshohocken Brewing Co, 3100 Horizon Drive, King of Prussia, PA 19406.

This is a free event. All experience levels are welcome. RSVP on Eventbrite so we know you're coming. The meetup is hosted by John "Johnny Mc" McAllister and is held on the second Saturday of the month.

Come with questions, deals, or a guest who wants to get started. This is a networking and education event, not a pitch and not a promise of profits.
