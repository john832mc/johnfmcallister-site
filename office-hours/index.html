Free Weekly Real Estate Investing Office Hours

Office Hours is a free weekly Zoom session with John McAllister. It's a place to ask questions, get unstuck, and map a path to your first deal, your next deal, or a more organized business.

When: Thursdays, 4:00–5:00 PM ET.

Where: Zoom, after you register.

Cost: Free.

Typical topics:

  • Pre-foreclosures and how that work actually gets done
  • Getting your first deal, or moving the next one forward
  • Using AI to run parts of a real estate investing business

Office Hours is educational. Attendance does not guarantee a deal, income, or a particular result.
