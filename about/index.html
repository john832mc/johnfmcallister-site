About John "Johnny Mc" McAllister

I'm John F. McAllister — Johnny Mc to a lot of people in this business. Through Asterias LLC, I invest in real estate in Eastern Pennsylvania and the surrounding area.

My work includes:

  • Wholesaling
  • Creative financing, including Subject-To
  • Fix and flips
  • Ground-up construction
  • Using AI to run most of the day-to-day business

I also host the monthly Eastern PA Creative REI Meetup in King of Prussia and free weekly Office Hours on Zoom. Those rooms are for investors who want practical help with pre-foreclosures, getting a first or next deal, and using AI — not hype, and not guaranteed outcomes.

If you want to follow the work, start with Instagram. If you want to meet in person or jump on Zoom, use the buttons below.

If you are a homeowner facing a sheriff sale and you want to ask about options, use the auction-help page. That request does not guarantee that a sale will be stopped or delayed.
